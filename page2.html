Generation 2: Gold and Silver and Crystal

These games follow Ethan as he explores both Johto and Kanto. In the Crystal version you can play as the girl option, Kris, for the first time in any Pokemon game. He runs into Team Rocket on his adventure and meets legendary Pokemon like Ho-oh and Lugia. Same goal as the last games but with a new region, more Pokemon, and a new story. These are the only games to feature two regions.

These games were originally on the Gameboy and Gameboy Color, but could also can be played on the Gameboy Advanced. These games were later remade for the DS with updated graphics, updated soundtrack, and updated battle mechanics. The remake is widley onsidered one of the best Pokemon games. They later rereleased Gold, Silver, and Crstal on the 3DS.
