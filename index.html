The History of Pokemon

Generation 1: Red and Blue

The first two games in the series are Red and Blue. It follows Red as he travels the Kanto region catching and battling Pokemon. Along the way he runs into legendary Pokemon like Mewtwo, and he has to put a stop to Team Rocket.

The characters

  • Red
  • Blue
  • Professor Oak
  • Giovanni
