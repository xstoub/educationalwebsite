The History of Pokemon

Generation 1: Red and Blue

The first two games in the series are Red and Blue. It follows Red as he travels the Kanto region catching and battling Pokemon. Along the way he runs into legendary Pokemon like Mewtwo, and he has to put a stop to Team Rocket. To become champion, Red needs to battles eight gyms, beat the elite four, and the beat the current champion.

These games were orginally made for the Gameboy but could also be played on the Gameboy Color and Gameboy Advanced. Later on there was a remake for the Gameboy Advanced with updated graphics, a new area at the end of the game, and new battle system. They also had another remake for the Switch involving elements from the mobile game, Pokemon Go. These games are the best selling Pokemon games of all time. They later rereleased Red, Green, Blue and Yellow on the 3DS and rereleased Fire Red and Leaf Green on the Switch.
